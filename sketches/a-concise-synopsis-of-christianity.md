# A Concise Synopsis of Christianity

## My understanding of the [[Bible]] and its tenets as taught to me in my early life

There is an all-powerful [[Eternal]] being, called, among various names, [[God]]. This God has a host of beings who reside with him in his [[Heaven|eternal realm]]. They have a hierarchy and roles.

God created the [[Universe|universe]]. This includes all [[Time|time]], [[Matter|matter]], [[Energy|energy]]. This [[Biblical Creation|creation]] was somewhere between six and seven thousand years ago. As the Creator, God has all authority over this creation, axnd may do as He sees fit with any of it.

God seems to have focused on [[Earth]]. He created the [[Land|land]], [[Sea|sea]], and [[Sky|sky]], along with the [[Sun]], [[Moon]], [[Star]], and other [[Heavenly Body]]. He also created [[Plant Kingdom]] and [[Animal Kingdom]].

Finally, he created [[Humanity|humans]], [[Adam the first man|Adam]] and [[Eve the First Woman|Eve]], in a [[paradise]] on [[Earth]] called [[Garden of Eden]]. God set only [[God's First Rule|one rule]]: Adam and Eve were not to eat fruit from a tree God placed in Eden.

- In the first story in Genesis, He created Adam first, then Eve. In the second story, there is no distinction.

At some point, one of the [[Lucifer|higher beings]] in God’s host decided that it was not fair for God to be preeminent and led a faction of the host in rebellion. For this, God punished them. The leader in this rebellion is known as the [[The Devil|devil]].

The devil, who was able to freely access God’s creation, convinced Eve, and thus Adam, to also reject God’s law. They ate the fruit, and this caused them to become aware and feel [[Shame|shame]] for of their [[Nudity|nakedness]]. God [[God's Punishment|punished]] them by ejecting them from Eden forever. He confirmed their nakedness and made [[Clothing|clothes]] for them from animal skins.

- Scripture does not here explain if nakedness was evil before they ate the fruit.
- No other knowledge either good or evil is expressed in the Bible for this case.

This changed things. Because Adam and Eve made a single choice, God punished all humanity with a [[The Curse for Sin|curse]] for all time. All people would [[Humanity's Suffering|suffer]] from [[Pain|pain]], [[Toil|toil]], [[Sickness|sickness]], and [[Death|death]]. One could choose to honor God by choosing to [[Worship|worship]] Him and make pleasing [[Sacrifice|sacrifices]].

- The implication is that there was no such thing as death until this incident.
- God placed extra curses on womankind, specifically painful [[Childbearing|childbearing]] and [[Woman's Place|subservience to a husband]].

From this point, God made it clear that he would choose favorites. When Adam and Eve’s sons [[Cain son of Adam and Eve|Cain]] and [[Abel son of Adam and Eve|Abel]] grew up, they each made offerings to God. God liked Abel's offering, which was animals, but didn't like Cain's, which was crops. This also set the expectation for all future sacrifices to God: He required the death and [[Burnt Sacrifice|burning of flesh]].

Humanity showed a proclivity to [[Homanity's Violence|violence]], and this grieved God. When he’d had enough, God decided to cleanse them from the earth through a [[Noah's Flood|global flood]]. He chose his favorite at the time, [[Noah from Genesis]], and instructed him personally to build a giant [[Noah's Ark|floating vessel]]. When the flood violently came, only Noah and his family survived, along with many animals he was instructed to bring along.

After the flood, however, God regretted his actions, and chose the [[God's Rainbow Promise|rainbow]] as a promise that he would never again destroy all of humanity. Then one of Noah’s sons offended Noah, and Noah [[The Curse of Ham|cursed him and his progeny]]. This curse established a racial hierarchy, though it is unclear if this curse was enforced by God.
